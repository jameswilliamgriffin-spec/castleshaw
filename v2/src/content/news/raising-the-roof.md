Castleshaw is one of 140 youth projects in England to receive a Youth Investment Fund grant, for improvements to the roof. Councillor Shaid Mushtaq, Cabinet Member for Children and Young People, said the centre “provides opportunities for people to enjoy activities they may otherwise have never had the chance to experience”.
