Write in British English, plain, warm, specific, a bit dry. No marketing cliches (no "unleash", "adventure awaits", "unforgettable", "immerse"). Sentence case. No exclamation marks except inside quotes.

Give me:
1. Eight options for the 2-line headline at the moment the illustration turns into real footage (currently: "Enough drawings. / This is the real valley."). Short.
2. Four options for the one-line hint under it telling people they can scroll back up to rewind.
3. For each of the 6 chapters, two options for a short heading (max 5 words) that fits the content placed there: Hills = where we are; Woods = plan your visit; Mud = puddle quote; Water = kayaking and canoeing; Fire = thank-you letters and drawings from children; Dusk = stay the night and get in touch.
4. Three options for the main hero headline (currently "Out here, the Pennines are the classroom.").
Mark your single favourite in each group with a star and say why in one line.
