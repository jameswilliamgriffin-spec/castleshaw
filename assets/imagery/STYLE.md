# Imagery style guide: Castleshaw internal pages

Generated imagery fills atmospheric gaps only. It never stands in for the real centre, its activities, its staff or its facilities. Real photographs always take priority.

## Look

- **Sensibility:** documentary landscape and nature photography, as if shot on a walk through the South Pennine moors on a real day. Close-ups and textures more than views.
- **Light:** natural only. Soft overcast, low sun, blue hour, or (Stay only) warm lamplight. No studio light, no dramatic god-rays.
- **Lens:** full-frame camera look, 50–100mm, shallow depth of field, real focus fall-off, fine natural grain.
- **Grade:** one restrained grade across the set: muted greens, peat browns, slate blues, stone greys, lifted blacks, gentle contrast, occasional warm light. Never oversaturated, never HDR.
- **Category colour families** (matching the homepage legend): Land green, Water blue, Bushcraft amber, Environment sage, Romans brick and terracotta, Stay warm neutral. Advice uses slate, stone and sage.

## Base prompt (prefix every generation)

> Documentary nature photograph taken in the South Pennine moors of northern England. Natural light, real textures, shallow depth of field, full-frame camera with a 50–100mm lens, fine natural film grain. Restrained, slightly muted, true-to-life colour grade: muted greens, peat browns, slate blues and stone greys, gentle contrast, soft lifted shadows. Calm, quiet, atmospheric, honest. Composition leaves generous clean space.

Then add: **subject**, **composition**, **light**, **colour family**, **aspect ratio**, **focal point position**.

## Negative list (append to every generation)

> No people, no faces, no hands, no feet, no legs, no body parts, no silhouettes or figures in the distance. No text, letters, numbers, signage, logos or watermarks. No buildings, ruins, landmarks or recognisable places. No vehicles, boats, tents, ropes, harnesses, helmets, carabiners, compasses or other equipment. No animals in focus. Not an illustration, painting, drawing, cartoon, vector art, 3D render or CGI. No fantasy, no surreal lighting, no heavy HDR, no oversaturation, no vignette, no lens flare.

## Checks for every image

1. No faces, people or body parts anywhere, including tiny figures.
2. No text, logos or numbers, including on stones or fake signage.
3. No warped geometry, melted textures, repeating patterns or fake-looking detail.
4. Mood and colour family match the slot.
5. Clean space where text will sit (quote backdrops: left and centre stay quiet).
6. Reads as a real photograph at 100%.

## Sizes

The tool outputs 1536 × 1024 (landscape), 1024 × 1536 (portrait) or 1024 × 1024 (square) PNGs. Banners and quote backdrops are generated landscape and cropped (21:9 or 16:9 desktop, 3:2 or 4:5 mobile); hero swaps and side images are generated portrait or square. Because the tool can't render larger, full-width banners sit at about 1× resolution on a 1440 screen; they are textures under a scrim or between sections, where that is acceptable.
