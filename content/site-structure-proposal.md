# Castleshaw Centre: proposed site structure (for the Version 2 build)

Based on an audit of all 20 pages on castleshawcentre.com (17 in the menu plus 3 hidden course pages), the EVOLVE service leaflet (2025), and a review of how eight other UK educational visits services present and sell themselves.

---

## 1. What other services do (and where Castleshaw can beat them)

| Service | How they present it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Nottinghamshire (Notts Outdoors)](https://nottsoutdoors.nottinghamshire.gov.uk/schools-and-groups/educational-visits-advice/) | One page under "Schools and groups" on its outdoor centre site. Legal duties first, then an academy "buy back" offer, Evolve and Evolve+, and a "Log into Evolve" button. | The closest model to Castleshaw: an outdoor centre that also runs the advisory service. Sells whole-trust deals. No prices. |
| [Coventry Outdoors](https://www.coventry.gov.uk/coventryoutdoors/homepage/7/educational-visits---guidance-and-advice-service) | Under "Schools" on the outdoor centre site. Credentials, a feature list (advice, Evolve, training, termly webinars, newsletters, policy support), free online training. | Adds termly webinars and newsletters for members. One email link. |
| [Sandwell EVAs](https://www.sandwell.gov.uk/schools-education/educational-visits-advisory-service-evas) | Two sections: service overview, training. Open to "Sandwell and beyond". In-the-field monitoring. Discounted training for SLA holders. | Explicitly sells outside its own borough. Named contacts. |
| [Enfield Traded Services](https://traded.enfield.gov.uk/thehub/store/enfield-education/educational-visits) | An online store product. Packages by school type and pupil numbers, each with or without Evolve+. | The most "productised" offer: clear bands, with prices shown after sign-in. |
| [Lancashire](https://www.lancashire.gov.uk/lpds/sla/educational-visits/) | Three downloads: service offer, SLA and policy. A per-establishment fee plus a per-pupil fee. EVC networks. | Downloads are the product, which is weak on the web. |
| [Telford & Wrekin](https://www.telford.gov.uk/download/downloads/id/26389/telford_and_wrekin_council_educational_visits_service_level_agreement_2023-24.pdf) | Per-pupil pricing with a minimum fee. Free for maintained schools (statutory), paid for academies. | A common pricing model. |
| [Lincolnshire](https://www.lincolnshire.gov.uk/directory-record/63804/educational-visits-service) | Contracted out to EVOLVE Advice Ltd at £206.88 per school per year. | Shows the going rate for a basic national-provider subscription. |
| [Hertfordshire](https://thegrid.org.uk/news/herts-offsite-visits-advisory-service-changes) | From April 2025 the role was outsourced to EVOLVE Advice Ltd (5+ advisers, email advice 5 days a week, 52 weeks a year). | Your real competition is a national commercial provider, not just other councils. |

**What this tells us:**
- **Almost everyone sells this badly.** Typically it's one information page, no prices, legal language first, a PDF or two, and a single email address. A well-designed, persuasive section would stand out immediately.
- **The usual package matches yours:** advice, Evolve, approval of higher-risk visits, EVC and visit leader training, policy support. Castleshaw's genuine advantages are the parts competitors lack or bury:
  - a **24/7 critical incident line** (even the national provider in Hertfordshire offers only a monitored email inbox);
  - **advisers who still lead groups every week**, from an outdoor centre an LOtC inspector called "extraordinary";
  - **in-person support** for complex visits;
  - **one free EVC place a year**.
- **Buyers fall into distinct groups, and the best sites speak to each:** maintained schools, academies buying back, whole trusts, and youth services. Several councils sell "and beyond" their own borough.
- **Packaging sells.** Enfield's bands (school type × size, with or without Evolve+) make the decision easy, even without public prices. Coventry's termly webinars and newsletters make membership feel like a community, not just a sign-off desk.
- **Existing customers need a fast route in.** Notts puts "Log into Evolve" on the page. Subscribers visit far more often than prospects.

## 2. Proposed site structure

Six sections replace the current 17 flat menu items. Educational visits advice becomes a full section, second in the menu.

```

1. ACTIVITIES & SCHOOL VISITS                 (for teachers booking a day or residential at Castleshaw)
   ├─ Adventure on land        climbing, abseiling, ghyll/gorge scrambling, hillwalking, orienteering
   ├─ Adventure on water       kayaking, canoeing, stand up paddleboarding
   ├─ Bushcraft
   ├─ Environmental education  primary + secondary curriculum, fieldwork, habitats, rivers, mapwork
   ├─ Roman history
   ├─ Team building
   └─ Plan your visit          booking, day vs residential, what to bring, information for group leaders,

   └─ Self-catering accommodation, catering, venue hire, accommodation-only bookings
```

## 3. The Educational Visits Advice and Guidance section

The aim is to turn a PDF into a section that sells to new schools and serves existing ones.

```
EDUCATIONAL VISITS ADVICE AND GUIDANCE
│
├─ Overview (landing)            the current v2 page, refined: promise, how sign-off works, what's included,
│                                24/7 line, advisers, quote form
│
├─ How it works                  the Evolve process step by step (visit leader → EVC → head → our sign-off),
│                                visit categories (which need our approval: adventurous, residential, overseas),
│                                review times, what we check, what happens if a visit isn't ready
│
├─ The service agreement         what's included, as a clear checklist
│   ├─ Evolve and Evolve+        the core system + the optional upgrade (MIS sync, e-consent, parent messaging,
│   │                            mobile registers) if you offer it
│   ├─ Packages                  bands by school type and size (as Enfield does), "contact us for a quote"
│   └─ Download the SLA          the formal document, for business managers
│
├─ Who it's for                  one short page per buyer, or tabs on one page:
│   ├─ Oldham maintained schools   what's covered as employer, and what's extra
│   ├─ Academies and trusts        buy-back, whole-trust agreements, one contact for the whole trust
│   ├─ Colleges and youth services
│   └─ Schools outside Oldham      "and beyond", with what in-person support covers
│
├─ Training                      EVC (initial and refresher/revalidation), Visit Leader, MoVE,
│                                head teacher and governor sessions, First Aid (ITC), MiDAS
│   └─ Course dates and booking  replaces the hidden Training Course Dates page
│
├─ Emergency support             the 24/7 critical incident line, what we do in an incident,
│                                MoVE training, a one-page "save this number" card to download
│
├─ Guidance and resources        policies, templates, OEAP national guidance links, checklists
│                                (public teasers, with full documents for subscribers)
│
├─ Our advisers                  Rod and Joseph: credentials, OEAP membership, inspections, survey results
│
├─ Questions                     FAQs (see below)
│
└─ Get a quote / join            form + phone + email, and an "Already a member? Log into Evolve" link
```

### What makes it sell (from the research)

1. **Lead with outcomes, not legal duty.** Competitors open with the Health and Safety at Work Act. Open instead with what a head teacher wants: safe visits, less paperwork worry, someone to call at 2am. Put the legal framing under "Who it's for" and "How it works".
2. **Name the advantages competitors can't match:**
   - the 24/7 incident line (a national provider offers email five days a week);
   - practising advisers at an inspected outdoor centre;
   - in-person visits;
   - one free EVC place a year.
3. **Productise it.** Packages by school type and size make the choice easy for a business manager, even when the price is "contact us for a quote".
4. **Sell to trusts, not just schools.** A whole-trust agreement is a single sale covering many schools, and Notts and Oxfordshire both sell this way.
5. **Keep members close.** Add an "Evolve login" link in the header, a termly EVC update or webinar, and an annual EVC network meeting (Coventry and Lancashire both do this). Members who feel looked after renew.
6. **Make training bookable.** The training dates page is currently hidden. Every course should have a date, a price for members and non-members, and a book button. Discounted member training is also a reason to join.
7. **Answer the business manager's questions** in an FAQ:
   - What does it cost?
   - When does the agreement run (April or September)?
   - Do we already have Evolve?
   - Can a trust buy for all its schools?
   - How long does approval take?
   - What happens out of hours?
   - Can you train our staff at our school?

### To confirm with the centre

- **Oldham maintained schools:** do they get the service as part of the council's employer duty, or do they also buy the SLA? This shapes the "Who it's for" pages.
- **Packages and pricing model:** per school, per pupil, by bands? When does the agreement year run?
- **Evolve+:** is it offered as an upgrade?
- **OEAP membership:** are the advisers accredited OEAP members? Some councils require accredited advisers to approve higher-risk visits, so it's worth stating if true.
- **EVC revalidation interval:** many services require a refresher every three years.
- **Member extras:** are there termly updates, networks or webinars, or would the centre want to start them?
- **Real figures:** the number of schools or establishments currently covered, and the annual survey result.
- **The AALA licence number:** L71687 or L137899.
