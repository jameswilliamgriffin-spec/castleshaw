## How it works

Every visit is checked by three or four people before it goes ahead. We are the final check.

1. **You plan the visit on Evolve.** The visit leader adds the itinerary, risk management, staffing, transport and any activity providers.
2. **Your EVC checks it.** Your Educational Visits Coordinator, a senior member of staff, reviews the plan.
3. **Your head teacher approves it.** The head teacher (or the head of your service) signs it off for your establishment.
4. **We sign it off.** One of our advisers reviews the whole visit against Oldham Council policy, national guidance (OEAP) and current legislation. We aim to review every visit within 10 working days.

### What we look at

- **Planning:** is the visit well thought through, with clear aims and a realistic itinerary?
- **Risk management:** have the significant risks been identified and sensibly managed?
- **Arrangements:** transport, accommodation, activity providers, emergency procedures, consent and medical information.
- **Staffing:** enough adults with the right experience and qualifications for the group and the activities.

### If a visit isn't ready yet

We don't just say no. If something needs to change, we send the plan back with clear advice on what to change and why, and we're happy to talk it through on the phone. Most visits are approved once those changes are made.

## Training

Training runs at the Castleshaw Centre, or at your school for groups.

- **Educational Visits Coordinator (EVC):** a one-day course for new EVCs and the staff who support them, in line with DfE guidance. One free place a year comes with the service.
- **Visit Leader training:** for staff leading and supporting visits, following the OEAP framework. A three-hour course, or two twilight sessions at your school.
- **Management of Visit Emergencies (MoVE):** for senior leaders who would run their establishment's response if something goes wrong on a visit.
- **First Aid for group leaders (ITC):** a two-day, HSE-approved course that meets most national governing body requirements.
- **Minibus Driver Awareness Scheme (MiDAS):** a three-hour theory course, at Castleshaw or at your school.
